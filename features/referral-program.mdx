Every **native.builder** account gets its own referral link. Share it with people you know — as they sign up, publish their first app, and move to a paid plan, you both earn credits toward your workspace's credit balance.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your referral link">
    Your referral link is ready to copy as soon as you sign in — no setup required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Send it however you like — chat, email, social. Anyone who creates an account through your link becomes your referral.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your friend signs up">
    They get a welcome bonus right away. If someone clicks more than one referral link before signing up, only the first link used counts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They build and publish">
    When your friend [publishes](/features/publish) their first app, you both earn credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They upgrade to a paid plan">
    If your friend moves to any paid plan, you earn a bigger credit bonus.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you both earn

| Event | Your friend earns | You earn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signs up with your referral link | +20 credits | — |
| Publishes their first app | +10 credits | +10 credits |
| Upgrades to any paid plan | — | +30 credits |

<Note>
  Referral credits are added to the recipient's own workspace balance — the same pool used for chat, code generation, and other AI-powered features. See [Plans and credits](/introduction/plans-and-credits) for how the shared credit pool works.
</Note>

## When rewards post

- **Signup bonus** — Posts immediately to your friend's balance. Unused signup bonus credits expire after 30 days.
- **First-publish bonus** — Held for a short review period before it's added to either balance. This gives Builder a window to check for fraud before the credits become available.
- **Paid-plan bonus** — Posts immediately to your balance, once per referred friend.

<Warning>
  Builder may hold or reverse credits earned through fraudulent or abusive referrals.
</Warning>

## Fair play

- Only one referral counts per new signup — if someone clicks multiple referral links, the first one used gets the credit.
- You can't refer yourself, including through a different email address or account.
- Your friend needs to be a genuinely new native.builder user.
- Referral rewards may be paused or held for review if we detect suspicious signup or usage patterns.
