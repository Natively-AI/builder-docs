The **Store** is where **native.builder** templates live. Open it from **Store** in the sidebar, or go directly to `/marketplace`.

A template is a full starting point for a new project. Use one when you want to start from something instead of a blank prompt.

## Browse and use a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Store">
    Click **Store** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse or search">
    Look through available templates, or use **Favourites** to come back to ones you've saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a project from it">
    Pick a template to open it as a new project. It builds from the template's starting point — keep directing the **Builder agent** in chat from there, the same as any other project.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Not every template is available on every plan — availability depends on your plan.
</Note>

## Favourites

Save templates you like to your **Favourites** view so you can find them again without re-browsing the whole Store.
